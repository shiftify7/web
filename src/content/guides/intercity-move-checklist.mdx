Intercity moves fail in the paperwork, not on the highway. This checklist keeps the boring parts on schedule so moving day is just physics.

## 4 weeks out

- **Fix the date window** and request quotes. Any firm price needs a survey — schedule video surveys with 2–3 movers.
- **Start the society NOC clock** at both ends if you're in apartments. Some Mumbai societies need a week; Gurgaon high-rises want 24–48 hours plus a lift slot.
- **Declutter ruthlessly.** Every carton you don't move saves packing, volume and money. Sell, donate, discard before the survey so the quote reflects reality.

## 3 weeks out

- **Choose the mover on the written quote**, not the phone charm. Lock the date, lift slots and gate passes.
- **Notify your landlord / agreement parties** per your notice period; photograph the current home's condition.
- **Begin the address-change list**: bank, employer, school, Aadhaar-linked services, subscriptions, DTH/broadband disconnection dates.

## 2 weeks out

- **Start self-packing personal categories**: documents, jewellery, hard drives, medicines — these travel with you, never in the truck.
- **Plan the car/bike**: condition-report slots if they're going on a carrier.
- **Freeze the furniture plan** for the new home. That wardrobe that won't fit the new bedroom is cheaper sold than shipped.

## 1 week out

- **Confirm the gate pass and lift slot** at both addresses; share vehicle details where the society requires them.
- **Defrost the fridge 24 hours** before move day; drain washing-machine hoses.
- **Pack the first-night bag**: chargers, toiletries, two days of clothes, basic kitchen kit, kids' essentials, documents folder.

## Move day

- Be present (or deputise one named adult) at **loading** to sign the inventory list — photograph the sealed truck.
- Keep the coordinator's number; you should get proactive updates at each transit milestone.
- At **delivery**, check cartons against the inventory *before* signing the receipt. Note exceptions in writing immediately.

## First 48 hours in the new city

- Beds and kitchen first; the rest can wait a weekend.
- Update gas connection, broadband and address-with-employer early — they have the longest lead times.
- Flatten and stack cartons; agree pickup with your mover or a local recycler rather than clogging the society bins (your new neighbours are watching, and so is the RWA).
