Moving gets messy when too many decisions wait until the last week. Set the date, write down what you own, and keep a small bag of things that never go on the truck. The rest is sequencing.

## Quick take

Most household moves settle down if you start two to four weeks out. Lock the date with both buildings first. Then inventory by room, pack the rooms you do not live in, and leave kitchen and beds until the end.

## Choose a moving date you can keep

Pick a day your old society, your new landlord and your own calendar can all accept. Month-ends and the March–July school-transfer stretch are busy in NCR. A mid-week morning is usually easier than a Saturday stacked with other residents moving.

Do not confirm the crew until you know:

- lift booking windows at both buildings
- where a goods vehicle may stand
- that the new keys actually work that morning

Write the date on paper you keep with your IDs. Phone calendars get buried.

## Make a room-by-room inventory

Walk each room with notes. Name the room, then groups of items — not every spoon.

A 2 BHK might look like:

- Bedroom 1: bed, two side tables, wardrobe contents, AC
- Kitchen: appliances, crockery, pantry cartons
- Living: sofa, TV unit, bookshelves, décor cartons

Photograph anything expensive or already damaged. That photo is for you. Give the mover a summarised list so the quote matches the house.

## Decide what actually moves

Every carton you skip is time you do not spend. Three piles: move, donate or sell, discard. Do this before you buy packing material. Carton count follows the inventory, not the other way around.

Clothes you have not worn in a year, leftover paint and broken gadgets are the usual discards. Furniture you would not buy again at a similar price is a candidate to leave.

## Build a packing timeline

Count backwards from moving day.

**Three weeks out.** Society permissions, survey, spare rooms.

**Two weeks out.** Books, off-season clothes, décor. Defrost a spare fridge if you have one.

**One week out.** Kitchen extras, linen. Confirm vehicle access with the supervisor.

**Day before.** Overnight bag, documents, chargers, medicines. Empty and dry the daily fridge. Photograph meter readings.

If you want the crew to pack, that has to be a line on the written quote. Do not assume “full packing”.

> Keep passports, IDs, property papers, medicines and daily electronics with you. They do not ride in the main load.

## Prepare both addresses

The new house should be able to receive boxes: power, water, a clear path from the lift, room names that match your carton labels.

If you are moving inside Delhi, Gurgaon or Noida, check parking and goods-vehicle hours the day before — not when the truck is at the gate. Locality pages on this site, such as [Dwarka](/packers-and-movers-delhi/dwarka/), exist because access differs street by street.

## Moving day

Before the crew arrives: valuables in your bag, fridge empty, keys and parking notes in one pocket, someone who can take decisions on site.

After the last carton, walk the old house with the supervisor — balconies, bathrooms, above the kitchen cabinets. Sign only what you have seen.

At delivery, count cartons against the inventory and note visible damage before the vehicle leaves. Make the beds and open the kitchen. The rest can wait until morning.

## Frequently asked questions

### How early should packing start?

Two to four weeks is enough for a typical home if you pack spare rooms first. A 1 RK can be shorter. A packed-to-the-loft 3 BHK needs more calendar, not more panic.

### What should I keep with me?

IDs, cash and cards, property papers, medicines, laptop, chargers, one change of clothes, keys for both houses, and the written quote with the supervisor’s number.

### Who should be present?

Someone who can agree an extra carton or a shuttle vehicle. A relative who cannot take that call slows the crew more than an extra sofa.

### What if the new house is not ready?

Do not treat the old living room as a warehouse. Ask for storage as its own line so cartons are inventoried for a unit, not a relative’s garage. See [storage](/services/storage-warehousing/) if that is the situation.

Planning a move? Share the route, the date and a rough inventory. After a survey you get an itemised written quote — no booking advance.
