A move inside NCR is short on kilometres and long on access. The truck is rarely the hard part. The gate, the lift and the loading hour are.

This is for people moving between Delhi, Gurgaon and Noida — not a timetable, and not a claim that any corridor is always free.

## Quick take

Treat an NCR hop as two local jobs glued together. Get permission at both buildings, confirm where a goods vehicle may stand, and count cartons even if the drive is short. Nearby still includes a ramp and a stack of other boxes.

## Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida — what actually changes

**Delhi to Gurgaon.** You leave NCT vehicle rules and enter Haryana society rules. Delhi peak-hour goods windows still apply at pickup. Gurgaon towers often have basement height limits.

**Gurgaon to Delhi.** A container may reach a Gurgaon service gate if booked. Delivery into older Delhi lanes may need a smaller vehicle shuttling from a main road. That is a quote line, not a surprise in the lane.

**Delhi to Noida / Noida to Gurgaon / Gurgaon to Noida.** Physically close does not cancel two RWAs, two lifts and two parking bays. Do not quote yourself a door-to-door minute count. Nobody honest can.

## Access, societies, lifts

Most organised societies want move intimation 24–48 hours ahead, resident ID, vehicle number, crew count, and sometimes a deposit you pay to the society.

Ask where a goods vehicle may stand, for how long, and whether Sundays are blocked.

Book the service lift. Measure the sofa against the door, not the brochure. If it will not fit, stair-carry should be priced at survey.

Locality pages such as [Rohini](/packers-and-movers-delhi/rohini/) exist because one sector is not another. Read yours if we have one; if not, ask security the same questions anyway.

## Dates and packing

Month-ends, school-transfer months and festival weeks are crowded. Mid-week late morning is often calmer — still confirm with both desks.

People skip packing quality because “it’s nearby”. Nearby is not a reason to mix glass with cookware. Inventory, labels, essentials with you.

## Both addresses

Old house: empty fridge, labelled keys, meter photos, society clearance.

New house: power, water, lift slot, parking instruction, floor space for the first cartons.

If keys are not in hand, do not load unless storage is booked. Sitting on a truck while a landlord delays keys is how quotes grow.

> Distance does not invent a missing carton. Count them at both doors.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is an NCR move cheaper because it is short?

It can be, but access (shuttle, floors, packing) often matters more than kilometres. The survey is how you find out.

### Do I still need an inventory?

Yes. Short hops are how kitchen glass gets left in the old society office.

### What about office and home in the same week?

Do not combine them on one vehicle unless the quote says so. Different buildings, different security desks.

Planning an NCR move? Share both localities and the date. After a survey you get a written quote that names access and packing — not a slogan.
