Hiring movers is paperwork discipline. You are not collecting slogans. You want a quote you can reread in a week and still understand.

## Quick take

Ask for the legal name on the invoice, an itemised quote after a survey, and the name of the person on site. If a number is “approx.” with no visit, treat it as a conversation, not a booking.

## Verify who you are hiring

Match the brand on the truck to the name that will appear on the bill. Shiftify publishes company details in the site footer. Write down GSTIN if GST is charged, a phone a person answers in support hours, and an address.

A social-media page is not a contract.

## Read the quotation

A usable quote names what is included, what is extra, and what would change the number — extra floor, extra carton, shuttle tempo, storage.

If packing, unpacking, AC work or a car moving with the house are assumed, they should be lines. See [house shifting](/services/house-shifting/) so you know which service you are actually buying.

## Pickup, delivery and access

The paper should name both localities, a date, a loading window, floor and lift. Who files the society gate pass — usually you. The mover can share vehicle numbers.

A large vehicle that cannot enter a lane is the wrong vehicle, not a bigger service. That should be caught at survey.

## Inventory and documents

Insist on a carton and furniture list. Photograph high-value items. Keep the quote, the inventory, the supervisor’s number, and the payment terms.

Shiftify does not take a booking advance. If another firm does, that is their policy — read it.

## Insurance language

If transit cover is offered, the quote should say what is covered, what is excluded, and how a claim starts. The word “insured” is not a blank cheque. We do not invent cover amounts in articles; whatever is offered sits in the quote.

## Payment, changes, communication

When is payment due? GST extra or included? What happens if the society refuses the date, or keys slip a day?

You should have one supervisor number for the day and a way to reach the office if that number fails.

## Questions before you book

1. Can I have the quote in writing with line items?
2. What is not included?
3. Who is on site, and how do I reach them?
4. How are extra cartons priced?
5. How is damage recorded at delivery?
6. What does my RWA need?
7. Do I pay anything before the move?

Vague answers mean you do not have a quote yet.

> Bring this list to the call. Seven ticks, then decide.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a video survey enough?

Better than no survey. A doorstep visit is better when stairs, parking and sofa sizes are unclear.

### What should I sign at delivery?

That cartons were counted. Damage, if any, on the same paper. Photograph it. Do not sign a blank satisfaction line.

### Can the price change after survey?

It should only change if the house, access or scope changed. Ask that in writing.
